## Excluded on licence grounds

`content/qworld/qec/` is licensed **CC-BY-NC-ND 4.0** (NonCommercial,
NoDerivatives). None of its images are reproduced here. This is why the
`quantum-error-correction` track currently has no concept diagrams — it is the
only track whose source imagery lives exclusively in that repository.

If error-correction diagrams are wanted, the options are:

1. Confirm the deployment is genuinely non-commercial and re-check the ND term
   (reproducing unmodified images is arguably permitted under ND, but the NC
   term conflicts with the paid tiers advertised on `/upgrade`).
2. Obtain permission from QWorld.
3. Commission or generate original diagrams, as was done for the first three
   tracks.

`content/qworld/qnickel-qcourse511-2/` carries no licence statement, so nothing
is reproduced from it either. Several of its images are duplicated in `qbook101`
under CC-BY, and those CC-BY copies are the ones used.
